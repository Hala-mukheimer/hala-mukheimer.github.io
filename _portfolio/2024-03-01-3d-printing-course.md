Completed a 56-hour training program with Digital Opportunity Trust (DOT) Jordan where we designed and built an Automated Environmental & Agricultural Monitoring System utilizing Arduino microcontrollers, custom sensors, and 3D printing for digital manufacturing. The project is fully customizable, open-source, and designed to support sustainable agriculture and environmental monitoring.
